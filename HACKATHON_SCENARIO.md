# BrandSentinel — Hackathon Presentation Script & Elevator Pitch

## The Problem
Enterprise marketing teams face an impossible tension: **velocity vs. governance**. 
Generative AI allows teams to create 10x more content, but brand review teams are drowning in hallucinations, overpromised performance claims, outdated specifications, and tone violations.

When an unverified claim like *"our platform boosts productivity by 98% for every organization"* slips into production, it exposes the enterprise to FTC scrutiny, legal liability, and brand degradation.

Traditional tools are either dumb spellcheckers or opaque AI wrappers that silently rewrite copy without explaining why.

---

## The Solution: BrandSentinel
BrandSentinel is an evidence-backed brand governance intelligence platform founded on one non-negotiable rule:
> **"AI recommends. Evidence supports. Authorised humans decide."**

---

## The 3 Key Differentiators to Pitch to Judges

### 1. Claim DNA — Marketing Claim Decomposition
We don't just flag a sentence as "risky". We decompose marketing claims into atomic parameters:
- **Subject**: Enterprise Workflow Efficiency
- **Numerical Value**: 98%
- **Baseline**: Standard manual review
- **Scope Gap**: The draft claims "every organization in 2 weeks", but our approved empirical study only establishes "up to 34% for Fortune 500 enterprise tier".
- **Golden Rule**: *A source supports only the scope it actually establishes.*

### 2. Brand Evidence Graph & Verifiable Provenance
Every single alert is linked via an interactive visual graph to an actual section-coded source passage in the corporate knowledge base.
`Brand Rule → Approved Fact → Content Claim → Finding → Reviewer Decision`.
No hallucinated citations. If evidence does not exist, BrandSentinel explicitly marks the claim as `"Unverified — approved evidence not found"`.

### 3. Review Diff Intelligence & Signature Cascading Propagation
- When a creator revises copy, our Diff Intelligence proves whether the edit resolved the underlying governance infraction or created new ones.
- When an engineer or compliance manager updates an approved specification (e.g., upgrading an SLA or deprecating SOC 1), BrandSentinel's **Risk Propagation Center** automatically flags previously approved corporate collateral that referenced the outdated specification.

## 30-Second Live Demonstration Script

1. **"Notice our Executive Overview"**: Point out the live Governance Health Score (88%) and real category distributions.
2. **"Open Content Review Studio"**: Show the 3-panel workspace with Northstar Labs' seeded marketing draft.
3. **"Click 'Run Governance Review'"**: Watch our hybrid engine detect the 5 deliberate infractions with exact character offsets.
4. **"Inspect Claim DNA"**: Show the decomposed 98% claim and the suggested safer wording (34%).
5. **"Accept Suggestion & View Review Diff"**: Accept the preferred term substitution and show the word-by-word diff verifying the resolved finding.
6. **"Switch to Approver & Sign Off"**: Demonstrate server-enforced role gating, mandatory rationale, and immutable guideline snapshotting.
7. **"Show Evidence Graph & Conflict Center"**: Zoom around the React Flow graph and demonstrate detection of contradictory active guidelines.
